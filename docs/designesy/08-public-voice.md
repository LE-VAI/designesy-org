# Designesy Public Voice Doctrine

## 1. Voice Purpose
Designesy public voice translates mission into clear, useful language without turning the project into generic AI SaaS, hype, or vague inspiration.

## 2. Voice Qualities
Designesy public language should feel:
- precise
- humane
- useful
- systems-aware
- creatively serious
- technically literate
- accessible without becoming shallow
- ambitious without exaggerating current state

## 3. Core Public Framing
Use:

**Designesy is design intelligence infrastructure for better worlds.**

Use:

**Designesy turns sources into principles, principles into contracts, and contracts into tools, systems, and artifacts that improve quality of life.**

## 4. Approved Phrases
Use approved phrases when they improve clarity and fit context:
- design intelligence infrastructure
- experiments that compile into contracts
- portable design contracts
- governed creative systems
- agent-ready design guidance
- better designed work
- tools for creative civilization
- public root
- system, not template

## 5. Avoid
Avoid:
- generic AI SaaS language
- “revolutionizing design”
- “next-gen platform”
- “all-in-one solution”
- empty futuristic language
- public namedropping of internal inspirations unless explicitly instructed
- pretending future surfaces already exist
- overclaiming scale, users, or capabilities

## 6. Tone Rules
- Say what exists now versus what is planned.
- Prefer concrete mechanisms over hype.
- Explain why a thing matters operationally.
- Keep doctrine readable.
- Keep public copy grounded in artifacts, contracts, Labs, and review.
- Do not hide behind vague words like modern, premium, innovative, or futuristic.

## 7. Example Hero Copy (Draft)
Draft option A:

Designesy is design intelligence infrastructure for better worlds.
We turn sources into principles, principles into contracts, and contracts into tools that produce better designed work.

Draft option B:

Designesy builds portable design contracts from real sources and tested Labs.
The goal is governed creative systems that improve quality of life.

Draft option C:

A system, not a template.
Designesy develops agent-ready design guidance that stays traceable, reusable, and operational.

## 8. Public Boundary
Internal lineage, private doctrine, and raw archives should be translated into original Designesy language before publication.
