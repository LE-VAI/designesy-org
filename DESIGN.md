# Designesy Design Contract

Designesy is design intelligence infrastructure for better worlds.

This contract governs how Designesy work should be created, reviewed, and extended.

## Core Rule

Designesy turns sources into principles, principles into contracts, and contracts into tools, systems, and artifacts that improve quality of life.

## Operating Principles

- Purpose earns form.
- Economy is intelligence.
- Context is part of the object.
- Systems enable freedom.
- Delight must be earned.
- Responsibility is a design material.

## Agent Rules

- Do not create generic AI SaaS output.
- Do not invent design decisions without a contract or source.
- Do not treat Labs as a blog or moodboard.
- Use reusable rules, not one-off styling.
- Preserve source provenance when importing doctrine.
- Separate observed facts from derived decisions.
- Verify visual work with screenshots or previews when possible.

## Current Status

This is v0.1. It is provisional and will evolve.
